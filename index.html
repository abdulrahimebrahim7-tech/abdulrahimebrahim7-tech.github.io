Projects

Smart Mirror (graduation project)

A mirror that shows you wearing clothes you choose, without trying them on. A Python interface captures your photo, lets you pick a garment, and saves the result. The try-on uses VITON-HD, and OpenPose finds your body pose.

Challenge 1: no ready dataset

No complete clothing dataset existed for this task, so we built our own: garment images and their masks.

Challenge 2: incomplete pose data

We couldn't find a complete OpenPose dataset. We collected data from several sources and merged it into one.

  • VITON-HD
  • OpenPose
  • Python

AI Student Services Chatbot

A chatbot for Damascus Training Center (DTC) where students ask anything about the institute and get an answer. It works as a student affairs assistant.

Challenge: no existing data

No dataset covered the institute's information, so we collected it and wrote it by hand. The result is a working assistant for student services.
